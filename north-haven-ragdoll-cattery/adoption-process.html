For Available Kittens

How to Reserve Right Now

This path is for families ready to adopt a kitten that is currently available. No waitlist is required.

1

Browse Available Kittens

Visit our Available Kittens page and choose a kitten marked Available that feels like the right match for your home.

2

Submit a Kitten Application

Complete our reservation application so we can learn about your home, lifestyle, and experience. Applications are carefully reviewed before a family is accepted.

3

Reserve Your Kitten — $500

Once approved and you decide to proceed, a $500 reservation fee holds your chosen kitten.

The $500 reservation fee is non-refundable once the kitten has been reserved, and it is applied toward the $1,500 purchase price.

4

Pay the Remaining Balance & Bring Home

After the reservation fee, the remaining balance is $1,000. Your kitten goes home at 12–14 weeks once payment is complete and all documents are signed.

Please Read Carefully

Important Terms

  • You do not need to join the waitlist to reserve an available kitten.
  • The $500 reservation fee is non-refundable once a kitten has been reserved.
  • The $500 reservation fee is credited toward the kitten’s $1,500 purchase price.
  • The remaining balance must be paid according to the agreed payment schedule.
  • The kitten will not leave North Haven until the purchase price has been paid in full and all required documents have been completed.
  • The adoption agreement must be signed before the kitten is released.
  • Once the kitten is transferred to the buyer, the buyer assumes responsibility for the kitten’s ongoing care and veterinary expenses.

Refunds

Refund Policy

Reservation

After Adoption

ALL SALES ARE FINAL. THERE ARE NO REFUNDS AFTER ADOPTION.

Once a kitten has been transferred to its new family, the purchase price and all amounts paid toward the kitten are non-refundable, regardless of a change of mind, change in circumstances, allergies, housing restrictions, lifestyle changes, or other personal circumstances.

Any applicable health guarantee is governed exclusively by the signed Adoption Agreement and does not create a general right to a refund.
