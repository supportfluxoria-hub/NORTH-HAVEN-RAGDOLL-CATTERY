Privacy Policy

Your Trust Matters

North Haven Ragdoll Cattery · Dallas, Texas

This Privacy Policy explains how North Haven Ragdoll Cattery (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you visit our website, contact us, join our waitlist, or apply to adopt a kitten.

Information We Collect

We may collect information you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Name, email address, phone number, and location
  • Details submitted through adoption, waitlist, or contact forms
  • Household, pet, and lifestyle information relevant to kitten placement
  • Messages and correspondence you send to us

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and process waitlist or adoption applications
  • Communicate about available kittens, waitlists, matches, and placements
  • Prepare adoption paperwork and provide follow-up support
  • Improve our website and family experience
  • Maintain records related to kitten placements

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only when needed to operate our cattery (for example, with service providers who help us communicate or process forms), or when required by law.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we encourage you to use care when sending sensitive details.

Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use basic cookies or similar technologies to support site functionality and understand how visitors use our pages. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Your Choices

You may request updates to or deletion of your contact information by emailing us. If you no longer wish to hear from us, let us know and we will honor your request where applicable. Waitlist and adoption records may be retained as needed for business and animal-placement purposes.

Related Documents

Placement terms are also described in our Terms of Service, Waitlist Agreement, and Sale / Adoption Contract.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at northhavenragdollcattery@yahoo.com.
