3. Purchase Price & Payment

  • The purchase price for a North Haven Ragdoll kitten is $1,500 (unless otherwise stated in writing).
  • A $500 reservation fee holds the selected available kitten and is credited toward the $1,500 purchase price.
  • The remaining balance of $1,000 is due according to the payment schedule confirmed by Seller before go-home day.
  • The kitten will not leave North Haven until the full purchase price is paid and all required documents are completed.

4. Reservation Fee

  • Once a specific kitten has been reserved for Buyer, the $500 reservation fee is non-refundable.
  • The reservation fee is applied toward the kitten’s purchase price.
  • If Buyer was previously on the waitlist, any applicable waitlist credit will be confirmed in writing and applied according to Seller’s waitlist and adoption policies.

5. Buyer Responsibilities

Buyer agrees to provide the kitten with:

  • Proper nutrition appropriate for a Ragdoll kitten/cat
  • Ongoing veterinary care, including wellness visits
  • A safe, clean, primarily indoor home environment
  • Companionship, socialization, and humane treatment
  • Appropriate litter facilities, enrichment, and grooming as needed

Buyer assumes responsibility for the kitten’s ongoing care and veterinary expenses after transfer.

6. Health & Documentation

  • Seller will provide age-appropriate vaccinations, deworming/preventative care as applicable, veterinary health records, and feeding/care guidance.
  • Any applicable health guarantee is governed exclusively by the signed terms of this Agreement and related written health-guarantee documents provided by Seller.
  • Buyer is encouraged to establish care with a trusted veterinarian soon after bringing the kitten home.

7. Refund Policy — Sales Are Final

ALL SALES ARE FINAL. THERE ARE NO REFUNDS AFTER ADOPTION. Once the kitten has been transferred to Buyer, the purchase price and all amounts paid toward the kitten are non-refundable, including changes of mind, allergies, housing restrictions, lifestyle changes, or other personal circumstances. Any health guarantee does not create a general right to a refund.

8. Transfer of Ownership

  • Ownership transfers to Buyer upon full payment, signed documents, and physical transfer/release of the kitten.
  • Applicable registration and/or pedigree documentation will be provided as outlined by Seller for the placement type (pet / breeding / show).
  • Buyer may not rehome, sell, or transfer the kitten without first contacting Seller, who may assist with responsible rehoming if needed.
