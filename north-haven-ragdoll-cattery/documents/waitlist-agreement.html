Waitlist Agreement

This agreement applies to families joining the North Haven waitlist for a future litter. It is a separate document from the Kitten Sale / Adoption Contract.

Important: Joining the waitlist does not reserve a kitten that is available today. To reserve an available kitten, use the Adoption Process and Sale / Adoption Contract instead.

2. Purpose of the Waitlist

  • The waitlist is for families seeking a North Haven Ragdoll from a future litter, or who do not yet see an available match.
  • A waitlist place does not guarantee a specific kitten, color, pattern, sex, litter, or delivery date.
  • Applicant does not need the waitlist to reserve a kitten that is currently Available.

3. Waitlist Fee — $300

  • To join the waitlist, Applicant pays a $300 waitlist fee. That is all required to secure a waitlist place.
  • The $300 is part of the $1,500 kitten purchase price and will be credited when Applicant adopts a North Haven Ragdoll.
  • Example: Kitten price $1,500 − waitlist fee $300 = $1,200 remaining when matched/adopted (subject to the Sale / Adoption Contract then in effect).

4. What Happens Next

  1. Applicant submits the waitlist application and, after approval, pays the $300 waitlist fee.
  2. Cattery contacts waitlist families when a future litter is planned or arrives and shares updates as appropriate.
  3. When a match is offered and Applicant proceeds, remaining payment and documents follow the current adoption terms, including the separate Sale / Adoption Contract.
  4. Kittens typically go home at 12–14 weeks after health checks and completed paperwork.

5. Refund Policy (Waitlist Only)

  • While on the waitlist, the $300 waitlist fee is refundable if Applicant withdraws before a kitten has been reserved or matched for Applicant.
  • Once a kitten is reserved/matched and Applicant proceeds under the Sale / Adoption Contract, reservation and post-adoption refund rules in that contract apply.
  • After adoption, all sales are final — there are no refunds.
After adoption: ALL SALES ARE FINAL. THERE ARE NO REFUNDS AFTER ADOPTION. Health-guarantee terms (if any) are governed only by the signed Sale / Adoption Contract and related written guarantee documents.

6. Communications & Preferences

  • Cattery will use the contact information provided to share litter updates and possible matches.
  • Stated preferences (sex, color, timing) are preferences only and are not guarantees.
  • Applicant agrees to respond within a reasonable time when contacted about a possible match.

7. Relationship to the Sale Contract

This Waitlist Agreement covers waitlist placement and the $300 fee only. When Applicant adopts a specific kitten, Applicant must review and sign the separate Kitten Sale / Adoption Contract, which governs purchase price, reservation (if applicable), care obligations, ownership transfer, and final-sale terms.

8. Acknowledgment

By signing below, Applicant confirms that Applicant has read and understood this Waitlist Agreement, including the $300 fee, credit toward the $1,500 kitten price, no guarantee of a specific kitten, refund rules before matching, and that a separate sale contract applies at adoption.
