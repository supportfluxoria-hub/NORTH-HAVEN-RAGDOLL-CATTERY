Welcome to North Haven Ragdoll Cattery

At North Haven Ragdoll Cattery, we believe raising Ragdolls is about much more than breeding beautiful cats. It is about raising healthy, well-socialized companions who bring love, comfort, and joy to the families they become a part of.

My name is Sallie Dubay, and I am a TICA-registered Ragdoll breeder with a genuine passion for this extraordinary breed. I founded North Haven with the goal of providing families with beautiful, healthy, affectionate Ragdolls while maintaining the highest standards of care and responsible breeding.

Our Ragdolls are raised with love and attention in a clean, comfortable home environment. From their earliest days, our kittens receive daily handling, affection, and socialization so they can grow into confident, gentle, and loving companions.

Our Promise

Our Commitment

Health, temperament, and quality are at the heart of everything we do. We carefully select our breeding cats with attention to their health, pedigree, structure, temperament, and the qualities that make the Ragdoll breed so special.

We believe responsible breeding means putting the well-being of our cats and kittens first. Our goal is not simply to produce beautiful Ragdolls, but to give every kitten the best possible start in life.

We take pride in raising kittens in a clean, nurturing environment where they are surrounded by human interaction and plenty of love. Each kitten leaves North Haven ready to become a cherished member of their new family.

For Families

Why North Haven?

When you welcome a North Haven Ragdoll into your home, you are not simply purchasing a kitten—you are welcoming a new family member.

We take the time to get to know our families and help match each kitten with the home and lifestyle where they can thrive. We also value the relationships we build with our kitten families and are always happy to remain a source of support as your Ragdoll grows.
