Wellness From the Very Beginning

At North Haven Ragdoll Cattery, health is not an afterthought — it is the foundation of everything we do. Every kitten is raised in our home with careful veterinary oversight, thoughtful nutrition, daily socialization, and the kind of attentive care that helps them grow into confident, affectionate companions.

We believe families deserve transparency. Below you will find a clear look at how we care for our cats before placement, what documentation goes home with your kitten, and how we continue to support you after adoption day.

Professional Oversight

Veterinary Care & Health Checks

Our kittens are monitored closely as they grow. We work with trusted veterinary professionals to evaluate overall condition, growth, and readiness for placement.

Before a kitten leaves North Haven, age-appropriate health checks help confirm they are prepared for the transition into their forever home. If a concern arises at any stage, we pause and address it — because no placement is more important than a kitten’s wellbeing.

Families receive veterinary health records so your own veterinarian has a clear starting point for ongoing care.

Preventative Care

Vaccinations & Deworming

Age-appropriate vaccinations and deworming are an essential part of preparing each kitten for life outside our home. We follow a schedule suited to the kitten’s age and stage of development, and we document what has been completed before go-home day.

  • Age-appropriate vaccinations, as applicable for the kitten’s stage of development
  • Deworming and preventative parasite care on schedule
  • Clear records so you and your veterinarian know what has already been done
  • Guidance on recommended follow-up care after your kitten settles in

Specific treatments and timing are based on each kitten’s age and veterinary recommendations, and are reflected in the health records provided at adoption.

Responsible Breeding

Genetic Health Testing

Responsible breeding begins with informed choices. Our breeding cats are genetically health-tested to help reduce known hereditary risks and support the long-term wellbeing of the kittens we raise.

Why Testing Matters

Genetic testing helps us make thoughtful breeding decisions with health as the priority. It is one part of a broader commitment that also includes temperament, environment, nutrition, and veterinary care.

What Families Can Expect

We are happy to discuss our approach to genetic health testing and answer questions during the adoption process so you feel confident about the companion joining your home.

Growing Strong

Nutrition That Supports Healthy Development

Proper nutrition is essential for growing Ragdolls. Our kittens receive quality food chosen to support steady growth, healthy digestion, coat condition, and energy for play and socialization.

When your kitten goes home, we provide feeding guidance and information about the diet they are currently eating. Keeping their food consistent during the first transition days helps reduce digestive stress and makes settling in smoother.

If you wish to change foods later, we recommend doing so gradually and are happy to offer guidance based on what has worked well for our cats.

Raised With Love

Socialization & Temperament

A healthy Ragdoll is more than a clean bill of health — they also need a calm, confident start in life. Our kittens are raised in a home environment with daily handling, gentle play, and exposure to everyday household sights and sounds.

  • Daily human interaction to build trust and affection
  • Litter training guidance begun in our home
  • Exposure to normal home life so transitions feel less stressful
  • Early habits that support the classic Ragdoll gentle temperament
  • Support as you introduce your kitten to children and other pets

This early foundation helps kittens settle more comfortably into forever homes and become the loving companions Ragdolls are known for.

Go-Home Day

What Leaves With Your Kitten

We want your first days together to feel supported and clear. Depending on the kitten and documentation completed at placement, your adoption typically includes:

  • A healthy, well-socialized Ragdoll kitten raised in a loving environment
  • Veterinary health records
  • Age-appropriate vaccination documentation, as applicable
  • Age-appropriate deworming and preventative care documentation
  • Feeding and care information
  • Litter-training guidance
  • New-kitten transition guidance
  • Written adoption agreement
  • Applicable registration and/or pedigree documentation
  • Continued support from North Haven as you welcome your kitten home

Specific inclusions and veterinary care details are documented in your adoption agreement and health paperwork.

Our Health Guarantee

We stand behind the care we provide before placement. Each kitten leaves with veterinary documentation, starter guidance, and our commitment to support you as they settle into your home.

Any applicable health guarantee terms are governed by the signed North Haven Ragdoll Cattery Adoption Agreement. We encourage every family to review those terms carefully and to establish care with a trusted veterinarian soon after bringing their kitten home.

Ongoing Support

Care After Adoption

Our relationship with your family does not end on go-home day. We remain available to help with questions as your Ragdoll grows.

A lifetime begins at North Haven — beautiful Ragdolls, loving beginnings, forever companions.
