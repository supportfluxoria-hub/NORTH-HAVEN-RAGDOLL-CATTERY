What the Waitlist Means

Join When You Want to Wait for Upcoming Litters

The waitlist is for families who are happy to wait for a North Haven Ragdoll from an upcoming litter — not for reserving a kitten that is available today.

When you join, we hold your place and keep you updated as future litters are planned, born, and grow. You’ll hear from us about litter news, possible matches, and next steps so you know what to expect while you wait.

To join, you pay a $300 waitlist fee — that is all. The $300 is part of your kitten’s $1,500 purchase price and is credited when you adopt.

If a kitten is already listed as Available and you want one now, you do not need the waitlist — go to our Adoption Process page instead.

What Happens Next

From Waitlist to Home

1

Apply & Pay $300

Submit the waitlist form and pay the $300 waitlist fee. That places you on the waitlist for upcoming litters. Your place is secured, and the $300 counts toward the $1,500 kitten price.

2

We Update You While You Wait

You do not sit in the dark. As upcoming litters are planned or arrive, we contact waitlist families with litter updates, possible matches, and progress as the kittens grow.

3

Choose Your Kitten & Finish Payment

When you are matched with a kitten from a litter, the remaining balance of the $1,500 (after your $300 credit) is due according to the adoption agreement — typically $1,200 before go-home day.

4

Bring Home

At 12–14 weeks, after health checks and completed documents, your Ragdoll joins your forever family.

Please Read Carefully

Waitlist Guidelines

  • The waitlist is for families who want to wait for upcoming litters and receive updates — not to reserve a kitten available today.
  • To join, you pay a $300 fee — that is all to secure your place.
  • The $300 is part of the $1,500 kitten purchase price and is credited when you adopt.
  • While you wait, we update you about planned litters, new arrivals, and possible matches.
  • A waitlist place does not guarantee a specific kitten, color, pattern, sex, or delivery date.
  • The $300 waitlist fee is refundable if you withdraw before a kitten is reserved/matched for you.
  • The kitten will not leave North Haven until the full $1,500 is paid and all required documents are completed.
  • After adoption, all sales are final — there are no refunds.
